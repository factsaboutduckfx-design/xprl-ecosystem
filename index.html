INDEPENDENT INFRASTRUCTURE

Validator center

An independently operated validator can participate in consensus, but it does not control the XRP Ledger. Other operators decide which validators they trust in their UNLs.

  • Provision a secure, dedicated Linux server.
  • Install and maintain a supported rippled release.
  • Configure ports, monitoring, backups and alerting.
  • Maintain reliable uptime and keep software patched.
  • Publish validator identity and follow XRPL community guidance.
  • Understand UNL trust: operating a validator does not guarantee inclusion on other operators’ lists.

HOW THE NETWORK WORKS

Consensus, in brief

01

Propose

Servers share candidate transactions for the next ledger.

02

Compare

Validators communicate and revise proposals over successive rounds.

03

Validate

A server considers a ledger validated when the required supermajority of its trusted validator set agrees.

The commonly described 80% threshold applies to a server’s trusted validator list (UNL); trust configuration matters.
